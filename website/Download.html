Build a slice

Pick the entity types you want, optionally filter to specific plants, and choose a serialization. Everything updates live.

How it works: pick one or more plants, choose CSV or JSON-LD, and the slice is generated in your browser from the open data — no server required. For the complete ontology (all entities, OWL/Turtle/RDF), use the Box download above.
No filter — including the full ontology.

Slices currently cover plants, their phytochemicals, and the links between them — generated in your browser as CSV or JSON-LD. Targets, therapeutic effects, clinical evidence, and RDF/OWL/Turtle export live in the full ontology above and are coming to the slice builder next.
