A knowledge graph of medicinal-plant pharmacology.

POPPy is the first resource to link plants, their phytochemicals, molecular targets, and therapeutic evidence into a single FAIR, machine-readable graph — every fact traced to its source, and built to be read by machines as readily as by people.

    What can you ask POPPy?

    Cross-domain questions answered in a single traversal — because plant, chemistry, protein target, therapeutic effect, and evidence all live in one graph.

    I

    Find a plant source for a target

    Which medicinal plants make compounds that act on a given protein or gene — and which of those are backed by clinical-trial or literature evidence.

    II

    Get a mechanism dossier

    For one species, its phytochemicals → molecular targets → therapeutic effects, with the source DOI or trial behind every link.

    III

    Spot convergent pharmacology

    Taxonomically distant plants whose different chemistries converge on the same target or pathway — alternative sources and ethnobotanical convergence.
