A knowledge graph of medicinal-plant pharmacology.

POPPy is the first resource to link plants, their phytochemicals, molecular targets, and therapeutic evidence into a single FAIR, machine-readable graph — every fact traced to its source, and built to be read by machines as readily as by people.
